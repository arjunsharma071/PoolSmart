Privacy, in plain terms.

PoolSmart uses your university email to sign you in. Google Maps verifies places and routes; Open-Meteo provides campus weather advice. Ride requests, account details, and payment states are stored to operate the service. A driver's location is shared while they are online or completing a trip. Riders can share a time-limited trip link with an emergency contact. SOS sends the location you submit and your emergency-contact details to the configured response channel. UPI and Razorpay payments use the selected provider. You can export or delete your account from Account; legal, payment, and safety records may be retained where required.

Do not use the service for emergencies. Call local emergency services if you need immediate help.
